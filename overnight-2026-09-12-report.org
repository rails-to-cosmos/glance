* A tag on an inbox jot moves it under the tag (=fix/inbox-tag-move=)
[[file:docs/bugs/fixed/2026-09-12-a-tag-on-an-inbox-headline-leaves-it-in-the-inbox.md]]
After a command's edits are known, a top-level id-less inbox row whose
edited title wears a tag run leaves: blob minted under the first tag with
the whole subtree (=mintedBlob=/=writeBlob=, the road capture takes too),
ledger noted, then the inbox cut under the request's digest. Blob first,
cut second; a refused cut after the blob landed answers =orphaned= naming
both. =add-tag=, =set-title= with a run, =rename-tag= inherit it. The answer
names the new id; point follows. Uniform over any inbox write, so =archive=
files a jot too — say if that should stay in the inbox.
