Refer — inline completion

The same verbs, drawn the way an editor draws completion: the list hangs under the caret, the pane keeps the keyboard, and the query is the text you are typing. @ links; C-u @ asks the kind first. Nothing is modal — carry on typing and the list follows, or ignore it and it stays out of the way.

@ link — target only, match required
C-u @ relation — kind first, then target
C-n C-p walk · RET or TAB take
ESC dismiss — what you typed stays as text
A bare @ fires only at a WORD BOUNDARY, so dmitry@example.com stays text. C-u @ is explicit and fires anywhere.
SELECT A REGION FIRST and @ turns it into the link — its own words become what the link reads as, and its text seeds the search. Nothing is written until you take a row.
