One <glance-table> custom element mounts the vendored table-view.js in connectedCallback from the org table text — Elm would render only this host and never its children. Both axes: n/p rows, f/b cells; f on the block enters row 1 cell 1, b at cell 1 climbs out. Both ways: those keys drive tv.select(id,col); a mouse click on a cell reports back through getSelection() (the event the glue would forward as a SelectCell port message). A link cell opens with o (tableview-link); a click on a header sorts the draw and writes nothing. Add: + on a ROW (no column selected — f once) adds a row; + on a CELL (f again into a column) adds a column after it. The point line says which — the row wash vs the cell crossing is the affordance; b off a row clears its highlight (the whole table, no row picked). Edit: RET on a cell edits its RAW text (link syntax and all); p above row 1 climbs to the column's HEADER and RET names it. A headerless table (the second one) has no header until p summons an EPHEMERAL one — naming a column makes it real. Judge both palettes with theme. Throwaway.
