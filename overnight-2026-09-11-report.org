* 2. The graph on the MCP wire
[[file:docs/proposals/done/2026-09-10-the-graph-an-agent-can-walk.md]]
- One edge relation per store version (=stEdges=, lazy, rebuilt through
  =withEdges=). Every =hrLinks= entry resolves through the row-name index; a
  link naming no row is no edge; a row is never its own reference. =refs=,
  =referrers=, =neighbors= and =ref:=/=from:= (both =*any*= and named) read it.
- =edges: true= on =list-headlines=, =get-headline=, =GET /headlines=,
  =GET /headline= (both shapes): =refs= [{to, kind, via}] with =to= a
  RESOLVED row id, and =referrers=. Without the flag the answer is
  byte-identical to before.
- =neighbors {id, depth?, kind?, limit?}= and =GET /neighbors=: the induced
  subgraph breadth-first, depth ≤ 3, nodes capped by =limit=, =total= before
  the cap. Nodes are the terse row shape. The MCP tool synthesises the route's
  own request, so numeric walls are inherited.
- =add-link {id, target, kind?, desc?, where?: body|title}=: twelfth
  =/command= verb through the one door, digest-locked. =LinkPlace= is a
  closed sum. Target resolves once at the door (=Asks= replaces =csDated=).
  Body road appends to the row's last own body line (=ownBody=: lifted
  regions excluded, =*bold*= is no child); title road composes =set-title=.
- Proposal's part 2 (=shape: rows=) was already in the tree; only =refs?= was owed.
